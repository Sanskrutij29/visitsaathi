The hour nobody planned for

Every plan assumes the visit will end on time.

Your mother's scan is on Thursday at 10.
Your brother can stay with her until 1. The cab home is booked for 1:30.
You're in another city, holding it together over WhatsApp.

Now the scan runs late. Who is with her between 1 and 2?

Nobody planned for that hour. The plan only covered the best case.

VisitSaathi would flag that gap on Monday, not on Thursday afternoon. Then it shows the options you actually have: a neighbour you listed, a later cab, or your brother staying an hour longer. You decide, and VisitSaathi drafts a short handover for each helper that you can share on WhatsApp.

Anything still unresolved stays visible.

How it works

From scattered messages to a covered day

No long forms. A few simple questions about the visit and the people helping.

1

Describe the visit

Pick the visit type and time, and enter a possible finishing-time range rather than a single guess.

2

Add your helpers

Sibling, neighbour, driver or caregiver, with the hours they can do and whether that is firm or tentative.

3

See the gaps

Steps nobody owns, shaky help on important steps, and times your parent could be alone if things run late.

4

Decide and share

Choose from options built only from the people you entered. You get a draft message for each helper to share on WhatsApp.

What it checks

The whole visit, not just the appointment

Most plans cover getting there. The gaps usually sit in the parts nobody thinks to assign.

Before

  • Appointment confirmed
  • File and reports packed
  • Who carries them
  • Hospital's instructions checked

Getting there

  • Pickup time
  • Who travels with them
  • Who meets them at the gate

At the hospital

  • Registration
  • Waiting and consult
  • Tests and pharmacy
  • Billing or insurance desk
Where the example breaks

Getting home

  • Return transport
  • Who stays until they are inside
  • Who is home when they arrive

After

  • Medicines bought
  • Reports collection date
  • Next appointment noted
  • Evening check-in

Why not just a WhatsApp group?

Keep WhatsApp. Add the part it can't do.

A family group chat

  • Only covers what someone remembers to mention
  • Plans around the visit ending on time
  • "I'll try" reads the same as "I'll be there"
  • Decisions get buried in long threads

VisitSaathi

  • Checks every stage of the visit, including the ride home
  • Tests coverage across the finishing-time range you enter
  • Flags tentative help on important steps
  • Drafts a message for each helper that you can share on WhatsApp
What we won't do

Honest about what a plan can and can't fix

No invented help

Options only use people and transport you entered. If nobody can cover a gap, we tell you plainly.

No medical advice

VisitSaathi handles logistics only. Questions about symptoms, medicines or doses go to the treating doctor.

No personal details

Helpers are entered by role, like "brother" or "driver". No names, ages or health information.
